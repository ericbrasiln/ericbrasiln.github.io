Short curriculum vitae

Associate Professor of the History of the Americas at the Institute of Humanities and Languages of the University for the International Integration of Afro-Brazilian Lusophony (IHLM/UNILAB), Malês campus, Bahia, Brazil, where he also teaches in the Bachelor of Humanities programme (since 2017).

Permanent faculty member of the Interdisciplinary Graduate Programme in Digital Humanities at the Federal Rural University of Rio de Janeiro (PPGIHD-UFRRJ), since 2024.

CNPq Industrial Technological Development scholarship holder (DTI-A) in the project Multiplatform ecosystems and attacks on health information integrity, since 2025.

Visiting Researcher at the Digital Humanities Laboratory of the Institute of Contemporary History, NOVA University Lisbon, Portugal (2022–2023).

Editor-in-Chief of Programming Historian in Portuguese since 2024.

Researcher at the Digital Humanities Lab – UFBA and UNILAB representative on the Intersectoral Committee of PopCiência Jovem da Bahia (since 2025).

Author of the book A corte em festa: experiências negras em carnavais do Rio de Janeiro (1879–1888) (Editora Prismas, 2016).

PhD (2016) and Master’s degree (2011) in History from Universidade Federal Fluminense.

Winner of the first and second prizes in the Sílvio Romero Prize for Monographs on Folklore and Popular Culture, in 2011 and 2020, respectively. Recipient of the PPGH Best Theses Award 2017, Universidade Federal Fluminense.

His research covers Social History of Culture, Digital Humanities and Digital History, with emphasis on abolition and post-abolition in Brazil and the Caribbean, carnival and citizenship, digital archives, computational methods applied to historical research, and the use of large language models and RAG architectures in Humanities research.

He worked as a school and preparatory-course teacher in Rio de Janeiro between 2007 and 2017.

Education

  • PhD in History (2012–2016), Universidade Federal Fluminense. Dissertation: Carnavais Atlânticos: cidadania e cultura negra no pós-abolição. Rio de Janeiro e Port-of-Spain, Trinidad (1838–1920). Doctoral research period at the University of Essex.
  • Master’s degree in History (2009–2011), Universidade Federal Fluminense. Dissertation: Carnavais da Abolição: Diabos e Cucumbis no Rio de Janeiro (1879–1888).
  • Bachelor’s degree in History (2004–2008), Universidade Federal Fluminense. Undergraduate thesis: Máscaras da Liberdade no Carnaval Carioca (1878–1885).
